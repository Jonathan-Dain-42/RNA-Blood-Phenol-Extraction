Post total-RNA Extraction DNase Treatment and Concentration

Adapted from R1013-5 RNA C&C Zymo Kit

Author

Jonathan Dain

Published

August 10, 2026

Purpose:

The purpose of this protocol is to remove gDNA contamination from RNA eluted using the Phenol-Phase-Separation protocol I developed. This protocol has been adapted from the Zymo Clean and Concentrator R1013 kit protocol to allow for use in the Virus+Lab. In addition this protocol has been adapted to allow for the use of TurboDNase instead of DNase I during the gDNA removal step. TurboDNase is according to Thermo Fisher a superior enzyme that catalyzes more DNA than DNase I and is more salt tolerant than DNase I which makes it a excellent choice for removing the carryover gDNA in samples extracted using a Tri-Reagent phase seperation protocol.

The printable version of this protocol can be found here.

Equipment Checklist:

  • 1 x Pipette-2 + Tips.
  • 1 x Pipette-10 + Tips.
  • 1 x Pipette-200 + Tips.
  • 1 x Pipette-1000 + Tips.
  • “Jake” the centrifuge on the lab bench.
  • Ice Bucket to thaw samples slowly.

Protocol:

Step 0A: Buffer Preparation

  1. Add 96mL of 100% EtOH (104mL of 95% EtOH) to the 24mL RNA Wash Buffer concentrate.
  2. If using DNase 1: Prior to use, reconstitute the lyophilized DNase I with DNase/RNase- Free Water and mix by gentle inversion. Store frozen aliquots. (See reconstitution table below.)

Step 0B: Tube Labeling

Note: To minimize errors and limit the time your RNA sits at room temperature, completely set up and label all tubes for your batch before removing your samples from the freezer.

For each sample in your current batch, gather and label the following five RNase-free tubes using a fine-tip permanent marker inside the qPCR hood:

  1. 1 x DNase Reaction Tube (1.5 mL): Label the lid with the sample ID. (This is where the initial 60uL DNase reaction will take place).

  2. 1 x Primary Elution Tube (1.5 mL): Label the lid and side with the sample ID. (You will transfer the Zymo column into this tube for the final 45uL water elution).

  3. 1 x GENEWIZ Tube (8-tube PCR strip): Label the lid and side clearly with the sample ID and “GW” (ensure this matches GENEWIZ’s specific online submission naming rules).

  4. 1 x Archive Tube (blue-0.5 mL): Label the lid and side with the sample ID, date, and “Archive”.

  5. 1 x QC PCR Tube (pink-0.5 mL): Label the lid with the sample ID or batch position number (e.g., 1 through 8).

Step 1A: DNase Treatment if using TurboDNase

Note: This treatment assumes a RXN volume of 60uL (allowing for use of the entire original eluent).

  1. Add up to 50uL of sample into a RNase-free tube. Add Nuclease-free H20 into the same tube to bring volume to 53uL.
  2. Add 6uL of 10X TURBO™ DNase Buffer to the same tube to create a final concentration of 1X.
  3. Add 1 μL TURBO™ DNase (2 U) to the same tube using a p2 pipette. Note that this solution is highly viscous so care should be taken at this step.
  4. Place samples on heat block set to 37ºC and incubate for 30 minutes.
  5. Proceed with RNA Clean-up protocol below.

Step 1B: DNase Treatment if using DNase I

  1. Place 40uL of sample into RNase-free tube.
  2. Add 5uL of reconstituted DNase I to the same tube.
  3. Add 5uL DNA Digestion Buffer to the same tube.
  4. Mix by gentle inversion or gentle pipette aspiration.
  5. Incubate at room temperature (20-30ºC) for 15 minutes.
  6. Proceed with RNA Clean-up protocol below.

Step 2: RNA Clean-up and Concentration

  1. Add 2 x volumes RNA Binding Buffer to each sample and mix. For a 60uL sample volume this would be 120uL buffer (assuming TurboDNase).
  2. Add 1 x volume of 100% EtOH and mix.(i.e. equal volume i.e. 60+120=180)
  3. Transfer the sample to Zymo-Spin IC Column in a Collection Tube and centrifuge at 12,000 rpm for 30 seconds. Discard the flow-through.
  4. Add 400uL RNA Prep Buffer to the column and centrifuge at 12,000 rpm for 30 seconds. Discard the flow-through.
  5. Add 700uL RNA Wash Buffer to the column and centrifuge at 12,000 rpm for 30 seconds. Discard the flow-through.
  6. Add 400uL RNA Wash Buffer to the column and centrifuge at 12,000 rpm for 1 minute to ensure complete removal of the wash buffer. Discard the flow-through.
  7. Carefully transfer the column to a new RNase-free tube.
  8. Add 55uL of RNase/DNase-free H20 to the column matrix and centrifuge at 12,000 rpm for 30 seconds. Keep the flow-through i.e. eluent

Step 3: RNA Aliquotting and QC

Note: This is a critical step and should be taken with care. Take a nice deep breath before pipetting to minimize error.

  1. In the labeled pink-0.5mL tube aliquot out 6uL of sample for QC. (1.5uL for nano-drop, 2uL for Qubit, and remaining 2.5uL for tapestation)
  2. In the GENEWIZ labeled tube aliquot 26uL of eluent and cap.
  3. In the archive labeled tube aliquot the remaining ~19-20uL of eluent and cap.
  4. Place GENEWIZ, archive tube into the -80ºC freezer in appropriate Cryobox. Keep PCR tube aliquot buried in wet ice for batch QC.
  5. Perform Nano-Drop, Qubit and Tapestation dilutions/assays on PCR tube aliquot for the batch before proceeding to the next batch.
  6. Once qubit concentrations are known, label a new 1.5mL tube and dilute sample to a concentration of ~2,500 pg/ul in 3-5uL for the tapestation assay.

Step 4: Clean-up and decon

  1. Dispose of all sample columns into the bio-hazard waste container.
  2. Clean workspace area with RNase-zap and then 70% EtOH to remove any remaining nucleic acids.
  3. Prepare the next batch of samples (as needed) by labeling the correct number of nuclease-free tubes.
  4. Record any important information in the tRNA DNase-treatment notes document as needed.
  5. Breath…..
